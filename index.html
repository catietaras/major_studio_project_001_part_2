About the project

A museum trail built from connections

George’s Treasure Trail helps young visitors explore Smithsonian objects through a five-stop, choose-your-own-path story. Each choice follows a shared material, topic, or time period.

What is in the experience?

  • Three starting objects located across the museum
  • Kid-friendly stories with expandable grown-up facts
  • A personalized treasure map and time-machine finale

Data note

Object details are based on Smithsonian collection records. Museum locations can change; visitors should confirm gallery availability with the National Museum of American History.
